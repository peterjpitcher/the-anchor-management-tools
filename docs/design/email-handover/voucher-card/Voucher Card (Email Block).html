The Anchor email kit · new block · 2 November 2026

Return-visit voucher card

One block, five variants, built on the dashed gold offer panel. Live text throughout, no images required. Production markup is in anchor-email-blocks-voucher.html, exact values in HANDOVER-voucher-card-nov-2026.md. Phone frames apply the kit’s 620px media query.
