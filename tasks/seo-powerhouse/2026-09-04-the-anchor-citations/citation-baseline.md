# The Anchor public citation baseline

Checked: 4 September 2026

## Answer

The Anchor has a strong, accurate Google profile, but the wider citation estate is not under control. The main recurring error is an old opening pattern which says the pub opens at 4pm from Tuesday to Friday and closes at midnight on Friday or Saturday. False claims about real ale, Sky Sports, a riverside setting and a play area are also being repeated.

The highest-risk remaining public records are inapub, Cylex, SquareMeal, Bing Maps and Yell. Apple Maps and TripAdvisor updates are awaiting moderation. Tabology ordering is retired and its residual public record is corrected apart from its old public email. OpenTable and Yelp are corrected. Google and the Food Standards Agency record are accurate. No exact duplicate of the core Google, Apple or Bing place was found.

Forty-five public surfaces, passive references and realistic placement opportunities were checked. The detailed register is in [listing-inventory.csv](evidence/listing-inventory.csv).

## Current standards baseline

The following is the safe standard to use later when changes are planned.

1. Use the real-world name, `The Anchor`. Do not put keywords, locations, opening hours or claims into the business name.
2. Keep one place record per platform for this physical venue. Merge or report exact duplicates rather than maintaining both.
3. Use the exact address, local phone and canonical HTTPS website. Link menu and booking buttons directly to the relevant first-party page.
4. Show customer-facing bar hours as the main hours. Use restaurant, lunch or kitchen fields for food service where the platform supports them.
5. Enter holiday and one-off event changes as special hours on Google, Apple, Bing and other supported platforms.
6. Use the fewest accurate categories. `Pub` should remain the primary category where available, with restaurant categories only when they describe the business rather than a product it sells.
7. Remove unverifiable attributes. In particular, do not claim real ale, Sky or TNT Sports, cryptocurrency, a riverside setting or a children's play area. Retain accurate platform-controlled dietary attributes, including OpenTable's `Gluten-free Options` and `Vegan`; use the approved NGCI wording and cross-contamination caveat in free-form venue copy.
8. Describe access precisely: step-free access is available, but there is no accessible toilet. A broad `wheelchair accessible` label can mislead if there is space to add detail.
9. Claim and verify the important listings, keep owner access documented, and review them after any permanent hours, menu, phone, URL or service change.
10. Do not buy bulk placement on hundreds of weak directories. Fix the platforms customers use and the records which feed other services first.
11. Do not describe The Anchor as independent. It is operated as a Greene King tenanted pub. Use neutral venue wording unless ownership is relevant to the platform field.

These rules match the current official guidance from [Google Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en), [Google hours guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/15300403?hl=en), [Apple Business Connect](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/business/abcbdc543423/web), [Bing Places](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/bing/add-and-manage-your-business-listing), [Yelp for Business](https://business.yelp.com/resources/articles/ultimate-guide-to-claiming-your-yelp-page/?domain=local-business), [Nextdoor Business](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-gb/getting-started/business-page), [TripAdvisor for Business](https://www.tripadvisor.com/business/claim-restaurant-listing-free), [Waze](https://support.google.com/waze/answer/6262592?hl=en), [HERE Map Creator](https://mapcreator.here.com/), [TomTom MapShare Reporter](https://help.tomtom.com/hc/en-ie/articles/360013960619-About-MapShare-Reporter) and the [Foursquare business portal announcement](https://docs.foursquare.com/data-products/docs/fsq-os-places-release-notes).

## Data relationships

Fixing a source record can remove several visible errors:

- Apple Maps displays TripAdvisor ratings and Yell content alongside its own place card.
- Bing displays TripAdvisor reviews and credits TomTom and OpenStreetMap for map data.
- Cylex data is repeated in FindOpen and may also feed Firmania.
- Mapcarta is built from OpenStreetMap.
- Restaurant Guru aggregates Google, TripAdvisor, Facebook, OpenTable and Foursquare signals.

This means Cylex, Yell, TripAdvisor and OpenStreetMap are more important than their own audience alone suggests.

## Unresolved account checks

These are evidence gaps, not confirmed faults:
